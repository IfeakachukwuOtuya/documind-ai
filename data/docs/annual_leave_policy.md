# Annual Leave Policy (HR-114)

Example Department. Fictional policy for training purposes.

## Entitlement

All full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays.

After 5 years of continuous service, annual leave increases to 30 days per year.

Part-time staff receive leave on a pro-rata basis, calculated from their contracted hours.

## Booking leave

Leave must be requested through the HR system at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need approval from a line manager and the head of unit.

## Carrying over leave

Staff may carry over up to 5 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-over leave must be used by 31 March or it is lost.
