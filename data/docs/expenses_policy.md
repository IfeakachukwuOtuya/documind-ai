# Travel and Expenses Policy (FIN-032)

Example Department. Fictional policy for training purposes.

## Rail travel

Standard class rail travel must be used for all journeys. First class is only permitted where a reasonable adjustment has been agreed.

## Overnight stays

The maximum hotel rate is 120 pounds per night in London and 90 pounds per night elsewhere in the UK, including breakfast.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense, with receipts attached. Claims submitted later than 60 days will not be paid.
