# Hybrid and Remote Working Policy (HR-207)

Example Department. Fictional policy for training purposes.

## Office attendance

Staff are expected to work from a department office at least 2 days per week. Teams may agree a different pattern with their director.

## Working from home

Staff working from home must use a department-issued laptop and connect through the VPN. Personal devices must not be used to access official information.

## Working abroad

Working from outside the UK is not permitted without prior written approval from the security team, because of data protection and security requirements.
